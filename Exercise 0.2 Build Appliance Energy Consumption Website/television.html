TELEVISION ENERGY

Understanding
Television Energy Use

Televisions are common household appliances that can contribute to everyday electricity consumption. Understanding the factors that affect their energy use can help users make more informed choices.

WHY IT MATTERS

What affects television energy use?

The amount of electricity a television uses can depend on several factors, including how long it is used, its screen size and its display technology.

01

Viewing Hours

Using a television for several hours each day can increase its total electricity consumption over time.

02

Screen Size

Larger screens can require more electricity than smaller screens, depending on the television and its technology.

03

Display Type

Different display technologies can have different energy consumption characteristics.

04

Extra Devices

Devices such as set-top boxes, game consoles and sound systems can add to electricity use when used with a television.

ENERGY TIPS

Using Your Television More Efficiently

01

Turn It Off

Turn off the television when it is not being used instead of leaving it running.

02

Adjust Brightness

Use a comfortable brightness level rather than unnecessarily high display settings.

03

Check Energy Ratings

Consider energy efficiency information when comparing televisions before making a purchase.
